The process

From adding a product to publishing it

You set it up once; after that you only approve.

🏪
Step 1

Add your shop and products

Brand name, tone of voice, shipping and returns policy, and products with real photos.

Everything the system later says to your customers is built on exactly this.

📊
Step 2

Product and competitor analysis

Your product photos get read, and competitor prices come off the open web.

Digikala, Torob and web search — the source and timestamp of every figure is stored so you can check it.

🎨
Step 3

Posters and images

Built on your real product photo, with the text laid over it.

Three modes: ad poster, social-ready shot, and cleaning up a photo you already have.

🎬
Step 4

Promotional video

Script, scene breakdown, and one continuous video with narration.

Five-second segments that each start from the last frame of the one before — one video, not a pile of clips.

✍️
Step 5

Captions and sales copy

For Instagram, Telegram and LinkedIn — each in its own register.

Three lengths, hashtags and a call to action, tied to the exact image or video it describes.

🚀
Step 6Done

Approve and publish

Whatever you approve goes straight to Instagram.

Nothing is ever published without your approval.

Division of labour

What stays yours, what runs itself

No decision that matters is made without you.
