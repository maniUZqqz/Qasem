NMarketing or a marketing agency?

Which makes sense for your shop — and when an agency is genuinely better.

An agency means handing the work to people who do this professionally. For a business with budget that wants no involvement, that is the right call. This comparison is for someone still deciding.

Side by side

AspectNMarketinga marketing agency
Monthly costA fixed subscriptionA contract, usually several times more
Time to startSame dayA meeting, a brief, a contract
Content volumeCapped by your planCapped by the contract
Understanding your businessFrom the data you enterFrom conversation and human experience
Unexpected creativityLimitedTheir strength
Final controlEverything waits for your approvalDepends on the contract

When the alternative is better

  • Long-term brand strategy — which does not fall out of product data.
  • Large multi-channel campaigns with a serious ad budget.
  • The creative idea no template produces.
  • When you want no involvement at all and can afford it.

When NMarketing is better

  • High volume day-to-day content, which agencies price steeply.
  • Round-the-clock replies to customers — agencies rarely offer that.
  • Starting without a contract or a meeting.
  • Content built on your own real product photos.

The short answer

If your problem is “daily content volume and answering customers”, NMarketing. If your problem is “what should our brand say”, an agency.
